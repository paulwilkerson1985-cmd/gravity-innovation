Blind screenshot comparison

You will see pairs of screenshots from a small pixel-art creature-battle game. For each pair, pick the one that looks more polished and professional as a game. Trust your first impression; there are no right answers, and some pairs are meant to look identical.

  • About 10 minutes, 23 pairs. You can stop and resume later in this browser.
  • Use No difference when you honestly cannot tell them apart or cannot prefer either.
  • Look at the screenshots at the size the page shows them. On a phone, tap A/B (or the image) to flip between them.
  • Keyboard: 1-5 pick, Space flip, Z fit/1:1, U undo.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. At the end you copy or download a small JSON file and send it back yourself.
